This is a general spectrometer built on a peg board to learn the science behind spectroscopy. It uses different components from Thorlabs and can be freely configured. Since it does not have a casing, it has to be operated in a dark room.

I built this spectrometer at UC-Berkeley's AstroTech summer school in 2026. I worked with Anaelle Roc, Parimala Rajesh, Grace Nasrallah, and Colby Moore.  

#### Components:  
- Thorlabs lenses  
- Thorlabs posts/holders  
- Thorlabs breadboards/screws  
- ZWO ASI 585MM Pro  

#### Cost:
The price of this instrument would depend on how many lenses and posts you used.
